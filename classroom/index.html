Watch the arm pick up the cube

Follow the cube as the fingers close. At what moment can you tell the arm has a secure grasp?

Google DeepMind’s recording shows a vision-based SO-101 policy. In this lab you will train the same pickup skill using joint and object state.

What is MuJoCo Playground?

  • The project. MuJoCo Playground is an open-source collection of robot environments and reinforcement learning tasks from Google DeepMind. Explore the version used in this lab ↗
  • The task. In SO101PickCube, a simulated arm learns to grasp a cube and move it toward a target. MuJoCo simulates contact between the fingers and the cube.
  • Your experiment. The robot model and training code are ready to run on your Mac. Train a baseline, change the lift reward, then compare the two policies: which one holds the cube up longer?

The hand went up. Did the cube?

Imagine watching the arm close its fingers beside the cube. The hand rises. The cube stays on the table. Follow this one attempt.

  1. Sense. The robot gets numbers describing its joints and the distance and direction from its hand to the cube. Those numbers are its observation.
  2. Act. The policy turns those numbers into commands for the joints and gripper. MuJoCo works out what moves when the fingers close and the arm rises.
  3. Score. The task reads the cube’s new position. If the hand rises alone, the lift score stays the same. The robot gets a fresh observation and chooses its next action.
cube_z = cube_pos[..., 2]

Read this line as “take the height of the cube.” The third coordinate is vertical. This is the measurement used to score a lift.

Where does learning happen?

Picture two attempts. In one, the fingers close beside the cube. In the other, they close around it and the cube rises. During training, both produce a record of what the robot sensed, what it did, the score, and what happened next. After collecting many such steps, PPO uses them to adjust the policy so actions associated with better expected returns become more likely in similar situations.

During a rollout: sense → act → simulate → score → sense again.
After a batch: PPO updates the policy → collect a fresh rollout.

The PickCube reward

rt = 0.02 × (Task + Control + Penalties)

Task

Which actions advance the pickup?

TermWeight
Reach1×
Grasp1.5×
Lift3×
Bring3×
Success2×

Control

How close is the arm to its reference pose?

TermWeight
Arm pose0.05×
Hand orientation0.05×

Penalties

Which costs reduce this step’s reward?

TermWeight
Action change1×
Excess joint speed1×
Joint limits1×
Floor contact1×
Each raw term is zero or negative.

Every 0.02 seconds, the baseline evaluates all eleven terms for each arm. A single step can earn credit for reaching and lifting while losing credit for an abrupt action. Each penalty already carries its negative sign.

Explore a term

Open a term to see its calculation and the Python that implements it. Distances are in meters. In the formulas, clip limits a value to the stated range and ε = 0.000001.

Task rewards

Reachreaching

exp(−(d / 0.05)²)

d is the distance in meters from the gripper site to the cube. Moving the hand closer earns credit even while the cube stays on the table.

Read reward_reaching ↗
Graspgrasp

exp(−(d / 0.025)²) × clip((0.80 − q) / (0.80 − 0.15 + ε), 0, 1)

q is the gripper joint position and d is the hand-to-cube distance in meters. Closing near the cube earns credit even if the cube slips.

Read reward_gripper_close ↗
Liftlift

clip((z − 0.018) / (z_goal − 0.018 + ε), 0, 1)

z and z_goal are the cube and target heights in meters. The score grows from the table reference to the target height, then saturates.

Read reward_cube_lift ↗
Bringbringing

exp(−(D / 0.08)²) if z > 0.018; otherwise 0

D is the 3D distance from cube to target in meters. Once the cube clears the table reference, moving closer to the target earns credit.

Read reward_cube_to_target ↗
Successsuccess

1 if z > 0.04 and D < 0.05; otherwise 0

Both conditions must hold on the same step: cube height above 4 cm and cube-to-target distance below 5 cm. The term can turn off again if the cube falls.

Read reward_success ↗

Control rewards

Arm posearm_nominal_pose

exp(−Σⱼ(qⱼ − q_home,ⱼ)² / 1.0²)

Compare the first five arm joint angles with the reference hover pose, excluding the gripper joint. Matching those angles gives a raw score of 1.

Read reward_arm_nominal_pose ↗
Hand orientationee_nominal_orientation

exp(−Σᵢⱼ(Rᵢⱼ − R_hover,ᵢⱼ)² / 0.5²)

R and R_hover are the current and reference gripper rotation matrices. Compare all nine entries. The score measures orientation even when the hand is far from the cube.

Read reward_ee_nominal_orientation ↗

Penalties

Action changeaction_rate

−0.02 × Σⱼ(aₜ,ⱼ − aₜ₋₁,ⱼ)²

Compare consecutive action vectors. Doubling a change on one channel makes its penalty four times as large.

Read reward_action_rate ↗
Excess joint speedjoint_vel_hinge

−0.015 × Σⱼ max(|vⱼ| − 0.4, 0)²

v is joint velocity. Only the portion of its magnitude above 0.4 contributes.

Read reward_joint_vel_hinge ↗

−10 × Σⱼ [max(Lⱼ − qⱼ, 0) + max(qⱼ − Uⱼ, 0)]

L and U bound the middle 90% of each joint’s allowed range. The penalty grows linearly with distance outside this soft interval.

−1 if any monitored robot part contacts the floor; otherwise 0

The contact sensor reports a single yes/no result for this step. Multiple simultaneous contacts still produce one penalty.

Lift high and hold

Add this function after reward_cube_lift in mujoco_playground/tasks/manipulation/pick_cube/mdp.py. The cube height is its world-frame z coordinate in meters.

def reward_cube_high_hold(features, cube_xpos_key="object/xpos",
                          table_z=0.018, z_cap=0.18,
                          hold_bonus_weight=0.5):
  cube_z = features[cube_xpos_key][..., 2]
  height_score = ((cube_z - table_z) / (z_cap - table_z)).clamp(0, 1)
  hold_bonus = (height_score >= 0.9).float()
  return height_score + hold_bonus_weight * hold_bonus

Next, edit the state-based environment factory in so101/task.py. Replace the lift term and turn off rewards for bringing the cube to its previous target.

def so101_pick_cube_env() -> config.Environment:
  cfg = pick_task.pick_cube_env()
  _configure_so101_robot(cfg)
  rewards = cfg.task_layer.rewards
  rewards["lift"] = config.Reward(mdp.reward_cube_high_hold, weight=3.0)
  rewards["bringing"].weight = 0.0
  rewards["success"].weight = 0.0
  return cfg

Each step at or above 0.1638 m earns a bonus of 0.5 before weighting. A cube tossed through the threshold can earn it briefly. Measure how long the grasp lasts.
